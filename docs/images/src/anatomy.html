@merainvoices/inr

Numbers the invoice

Financial year 2026-27 from the date · 15 of the 16 characters allowed.

@merainvoices/gstin

Checks the buyer's GSTIN

Valid · Maharashtra · same state as the seller, so CGST + SGST.

@merainvoices/gst-invoice

Adds it up, to the paisa

Line discounts, freight with its own GST, the CGST/SGST split and round-off −0.38. The lines always match the totals.

@merainvoices/inr

Writes the amount in words

In lakh and crore, as Indian invoices print it.
